🏆 1.75L Handle Strategy for 6–8 Adults & 30–40oz Tumblers (Standard Retail Spirit Handles):

For 6–8 adults drinking 2–4 drinks per day in 30–40oz Yeti mugs with heavy ice, standard 750ml bottles run dry in 1 afternoon! Stocking 1.75L handles of core workhorse spirits slashes alcohol costs by 30%–48% per ounce compared to 750ml fifths, giving your group ample volume for double beach pours all week without buying duplicate bottles:

🥃
Tequila (1.75L Handle): Espolòn 100% Blue Agave (~$48.99) — 100% Blue Weber Agave workhorse; saves $50+ vs Casamigos handle ($99+) with pure agave taste
🍸
Vodka (1.75L Handle): Tito's Handmade Vodka (~$29.99) — 6x-distilled corn vodka; handle cuts cost to $0.51/oz (vs $0.87/oz in 750ml) for 30–40oz Yeti coolers
🌴
Rum (1.75L Handles): Bacardi Superior (~$21.99) & Myers's Dark (~$39.99) — Bulk 1.75L pairing for all Back Porch punches, buckets & dark rum floats
🥥
Coconut Rum (1.75L Handle): Malibu Caribbean Rum (~$24.99) — Essential tropical coconut base for 30–40oz Yeti mugs, porch punch & beach buckets
🍀
Irish Whiskey (1.75L Handle): Bushmills Original (~$54.39) or Jameson (~$44.99) — McGuire's authentic house pour in a full 1.75L handle for iced coffees & mules
🍊
Orange Liqueur (1.0L Bottle): DeKuyper Triple Sec 48° (~$10.99) — 1.0-Liter bottle saves $27+ vs Grand Marnier/Cointreau with great citrus sweetness
☕
Coffee & Cream (1.75L Handles): Kahlúa (~$52.99) & Carolans Irish Cream (~$26.99) — Carolans handle saves $20 vs Baileys 1.75L with real honey & cream
🍇
Cordials: DeKuyper Peachtree (1.0L ~$13.99), Blue Curaçao (750ml ~$11.99), Dark Crème de Cacao (750ml ~$11.99) — Exact specialty notes from 1 brand line
