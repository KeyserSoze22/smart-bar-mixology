🚤 Thursday Boat Excursion: Double Fun Pontoons to Crab Island

Dedicated guide for Thursday, Sept 24 • Cruising on Double Fun Watersports 30' Double-Decker Pontoon with Dual Waterslides & Floating Lily Pad • Full Day: 9:30 AM – 5:00 PM (departs Destin Harbor docks, 3 mins from The Islander!)

⚓ Exclusively Booked: Double Fun 9:30 AM – 5:00 PM

🚢 Selected Vessel: Double Fun Double-Decker

You have chosen Double Fun Watersports! While standard pontoon rentals in Destin are capped at 12 people, Double Fun's double-deckers are USCG-rated for up to 14 passengers — giving your group of 12 (10 adults + 2 kids) plenty of room to stretch out with large beach coolers and dry bags.

  • Two Waterslides off the Top Deck: The ultimate highlight! Two curved fiberglass slides leading from the upper sundeck directly into the shallow emerald water at Crab Island. The 2 kids (and adults!) can slide all afternoon.
  • FREE Giant Floating Lily Pad Mat: Double Fun includes a massive 6' x 18' heavy-duty foam Lily Pad splash mat with your rental at no extra fee (saving $40–$60 compared to other outfitters). Tether it off the back of the pontoon for an instant floating island!
  • Dual-Deck Layout & Bluetooth Sound: Upper open-air sundeck for tanning and sliding, plus a full covered lower lounge with plush wrap-around sofa seating to stay cool in the shade. Features high-output Bluetooth audio to play your family beach playlist.
  • Driver Info: Anyone born before Jan 1, 1988 can drive with a regular driver's license. Anyone born on or after Jan 1, 1988 can drive after taking a quick 15-minute Florida Boaters Safety temporary exam online (or book a licensed captain charter).

🏝️ Crab Island Destination & Tides

Crab Island is Destin's world-famous shallow underwater sandbar located just north of the Marler Bridge in Choctawhatchee Bay (10 min boat ride from harbor docks).

  • Late September Advantage: Shoulder season brings the best conditions of the year! Peak summer crowds are gone (no boat-to-boat wall-to-wall rafting), parking at marinas is effortless, and water clarity is crystal clear.
  • High Tide Emerald Clarity: Water is waist-deep (2 to 4 feet) over sugar-white sand. Perfect for standing, tossing a football, floating on noodles, or letting the kids splash safely.
  • Floating Snack Boats & Music: Mobile vendor barges operate at Crab Island offering ice cream, fresh coconuts, grilled burgers, and inflatable water parks for kids (bring cash or cards!).
  • Dolphin Watching in the Pass: Keep your eyes open on the 10-minute cruise through Destin Harbor and the East Pass — local bottlenose dolphins feed and play along the jetties almost daily!

🧺 Cooler Packing & Grocery Integration

Tie directly into your 6-Person Lunch & Snack Grocery Checklist below to pack the boat cooler before departure:

  • Ziploc Sandwich Prep: Pre-assemble turkey/cheese and ham/cheese sandwiches on Hawaiian rolls and seal them in heavy-duty gallon Ziploc freezer bags so melting cooler ice doesn't make bread soggy.
  • Refreshing Frozen Snacks: Pack the frozen green grapes, pre-cut watermelon chunks, string cheese, and individual chip bags from your checklist for easy snacking without sandy fingers.
  • Condo Drink Prep: Mix a pitcher of The Ultimate Back Porch Punch or Emerald Coast Painkillers (recipes below) into insulated YETI jugs, plus canned beers/seltzers, Capri Suns for the kids, and plenty of bottled water.
  • 🚨 STRICT DESTIN NO-GLASS RULE: Okaloosa County Sheriff and FWC actively patrol Crab Island and the harbor. Absolutely NO GLASS bottles or containers are permitted on the water ($500 fine). Use Solo cups and cans only!

📋 Destin Survival Guide: Party of 12 (Audited for Sept 20–26)

🎯 1. The "Two Tables of 6" Hack

  • For a party of 12, many restaurants have only 1 or 2 tables large enough, leading to long waits even in September.
  • Ask the host if they can seat two tables of 6 side-by-side or back-to-back (5 adults + 1 kid per table).
  • This cuts wait times dramatically (often down to 15–25 minutes in shoulder season) and makes conversation and bill splitting much easier!

💵 2. Destin Gratuity & Check Policies

  • Parties of 7 or more in Destin are almost always assessed an automatic 18% to 20% gratuity.
  • Check your itemized bill before adding tips to prevent accidental double-tipping.
  • Let the server know before ordering drinks if you want separate checks (e.g. by couples or households).

🎪 3. Navigating the Seafood Festival Weekend

  • The Destin Seafood Festival begins Friday afternoon, Sept 25.
  • If you want festival fun: Go on Friday afternoon to walk the harbor, hear live music, and let the kids explore the free boardwalk.
  • If you want a sit-down family dinner without festival traffic on Friday/Saturday: Head east to The Ocean Club or The Crab Trap.

⏰ 4. Exact Best Times for Your Week

  • Lunch Window: Arrive at 11:15 AM or after 1:30 PM (great for McGuire's $10.99 specials).
  • Dinner Peak: 6:00 PM – 7:45 PM.
  • The Sweet Spot: 4:45 PM – 5:00 PM. Seated immediately, unlocks 5-free-entrees BOGO deals, and finishes right in time for the 6:46 PM sunset!
